# Earpiece options

Hypothetical: no audiogram was given, so every option uses throat 25 mm2 and mouth 150 mm2.
Scale assumes a 62 mm tall ear (no coin in the photos). Print in flexible resin or cast silicone.
Not a medical device; nothing here predicts hearing benefit yet.

## Ear A - photo 1, straight hair, LEFT ear

- **A1. Invisible** (`EarA/A1_invisible.stl`): flush faceplate, no collector. Someone who wears it all day at work and wants nobody to notice it. Sticks out 5.9 mm; prints at 2.55 cc.
- **A3. Side companion** (`EarA/A3_side-companion.stl`): straight sideways-facing trumpet bell. Someone who walks or drives with a companion on this side and wants sound gathered from that direction. Sticks out 18.1 mm; prints at 3.65 cc.

## Ear B - photo 2, close-up with earring, RIGHT ear

- **B4. Group room** (`EarB/B4_group-room.stl`): low wide even bowl, open toward the front. Someone in meetings and classes with voices coming from many directions in front. Sticks out 12.2 mm; prints at 2.95 cc.
- **B5. Maximum reach** (`EarB/B5_maximum-reach.stl`): long flaring trumpet bell leaning toward the front. Someone who listens to lectures and TV across a room and does not mind a visible device. Sticks out 33.3 mm; prints at 4.43 cc.

## Ear C - screenshot, curly hair, LEFT ear

- **C2. Table talk** (`EarC/C2_table-talk.stl`): small cone with a tall back lip, leaning forward. Someone at dinners and one-on-one talks with the speaker in front, who wants some gain without a big device. Sticks out 21.8 mm; prints at 3.44 cc.
- **C4. Group room** (`EarC/C4_group-room.stl`): low, wide, flattened petal-like dish. Someone in meetings and classes with voices from many directions in front, who wants an even pickup. Sticks out 11.1 mm; prints at 3.31 cc.

## Sizes

Each pick comes in three sizes. M is the cast of the photo-built ear; S is 0.5 mm narrower all
round, L is 0.5 mm wider (height and the bore are the same in all three). Print all three and keep
the one that seats snugly without pressure. A3's L size has its removal tab at the back edge
instead of the upper back, because at L size the tab touched the ear.
